## What it does

- **Share to Fetchly.** In YouTube, TikTok, Instagram or X, tap **Share → Fetchly**. No copying and pasting.
- **Spots copied links.** Copied a link? Fetchly offers to download it as soon as you open the app.
- **Pick the format.** MP4 video in the quality you choose, or audio as MP3 (up to 320 kbps) or M4A.
- **Saves to your gallery.** Videos go straight into your Gallery. Audio opens the share sheet so you can save it to Files or Music, or send it.
- **Keeps working in the background.** A notification shows the progress while you use other apps.
- **Download history.** Play, share or delete everything you've saved. The history stays on your phone.

## How to install

1. **Download the APK on your phone** using the link above. If Chrome warns that the file may be harmful, tap **Download anyway**. Android shows this for every app that doesn't come from Google Play.
2. **Open the downloaded file.** Android asks you to allow Chrome (or your file manager) to **install unknown apps**. Turn it on for this install. You can turn it off again afterwards.
3. **Tap Install, then Open.** Allow notifications to see download progress, and allow access to photos and videos so Fetchly can save to your gallery.

**Updating:** download the new APK and install it over the old one. Your settings and download history stay.

## Is it safe?

- **Why isn't it on Google Play?** Google doesn't allow apps that download from YouTube, so the full app is shared here instead.
- **Only download Fetchly from this page or [the Fetchly website](https://fetchly.sethboadu391.workers.dev/app).** Copies from other sites may have been changed.
- **Check the file if you like.** Every release lists the APK's SHA-256 checksum, so you can confirm your copy is the original.
- **Permissions:** notifications (download progress) and photos/videos (only to save your downloads). Fetchly never asks for your contacts, microphone or location.

## Privacy

There's no account. The links you paste are sent to the Fetchly server, which fetches the file for you. Finished files are deleted from the server after 30 minutes, and your download history is stored only on your phone. Details: [Privacy Policy](https://fetchly.sethboadu391.workers.dev/privacy).
